## Seraphina

[[1 Index/Table of Contents\|Table of Contents]]
[[Gods/Gods\|Gods]]

### Overview
Seraphina, revered as the Mistress of the Weave, is a goddess of unparalleled grace and wisdom.  She is the embodiment of magic itself, governing the intricate and invisible threads that connect all magical forces. Known for her calm demeanor and elegant presence, Seraphina is a guiding light for those who seek to understand the arcane. Her teachings emphasize the importance of inner peace and clarity of mind in mastering the arcane arts. She encourages her followers to approach magic with respect and reverence, guiding them to weave spells with precision and grace. Those who do not respect magic are often shunned by her, receiving no support or knowledge from her. In some cases they may even be cut off completely from the weave. 

### Characteristics
- **The Enchanted Veil**: Her divine realm, a sanctuary where the secrets of the weave are revealed to those who seek knowledge with a pure heart.
- **Weave-keepers**: Seraphina's most devout followers, handpicked by her to reside in the Enchanted Veil to study magic alongside her and preserve magical knowledge. At times they will leave the realm to educate creatures on the ways of magic and the weave; taking them on as an apprentice.
- **Elegance**: Seraphina is known to always extrude elegance. Her grace is mesmerizing. She is always tactful in her communications and holds herself high with confidence. 
- **Wise**: She has wonderful insight and foresight, sometimes even seeing the future itself in visions. She has a high level of wisdom, leading her to have strict discipline in the ways that magic should be used.
- **Empathetic**: She is one of the more empathetic gods, if not the most empathetic. While she is strict in the ways of magic, she is not without a caring heart. Often she has offered magic to those who are in dire situations and need a helping hand. Many who come to her seeking guidance or help usually leave satisfied, so long as they are honest and they respect magic. She will also create Warlock pacts with those she deems worthy enough of the power or in desperate need of help for a righteous cause. However, she prefers naturally born magic users, feeling they have a much deeper and purer connection to the weave. 
- **Good and Evil**: Due to her compassionate heart, those who use magic for wrong doing and have little respect for the power they wield are often finding themselves cut off from her guidance. In some extreme cases she has blocked creatures from the weave entirely, leaving them to have to find power and magic from other sources. However, she does not enjoy doing this, preferring to offer help to those suffering from power misuse so that they may solve the problem themselves; to grow and learn from it all.
