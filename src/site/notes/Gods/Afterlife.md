## Afterlife

[[1 Index/Table of Contents\|Table of Contents]]
[[Gods/Elara & Nyxara\|Elara & Nyxara]]
Nxyara's Realm. The afterlife is filled with all those who have passed, it is composed of 2 levels. 
The first level is vast and empty plains with few structures for souls to wander around aimlessly, offering nothing but talking to fellow souls. Souls will stay in the level for 100 years or forever if they are one of Elara's chosen and Lifeborn. She keeps souls there for 100 years to be sure they will not be selected as one of Elara's chose. 
The second level is for those who have been in the afterlife for 100 years or are Shadowborn. This level serves as a mirror realm to the land of the living but far more luxurious. There is never a shortage of any supplies or food. It is a lawless domain for souls to spend the rest of eternity doing as they please. 
The entirety of the Afterlife is hued in grey, colors seeming muted. Even the sounds seem muffled.
