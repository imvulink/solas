## Thalor 

[[1 Index/Table of Contents\|Table of Contents]]
[[Gods/Gods\|Gods]]
![Nature.png](/img/user/Nature.png)
### Overview
Thalor embodies the intrinsic connection between nature, its creatures, and the elemental forces. Known for his kind and steadfast nature, Thalor serves as a guardian and guide, ensuring the harmony and balance of the natural world and its primal energies. rimary role is the preservation and protection of nature and its inhabitants. His kindness extends to all creatures, great and small, and his steadfast commitment to the natural order is unwavering. Through him, the harmony of nature and the elements is celebrated, and the enduring spirit of the wild is safeguarded for generations to come.
### Characteristics
- **Druids**: Thalor's teachings emphasize the interconnectedness of all life, urging  druids to honor the elements and protect the natural world. He demands that nature be protected at all costs, even if the price is great. 
- **Kind**: Thalor is known to be kind and gentle to all creatures, but most specifically animals and beasts. He protects and values all flora and fauna, ensuring their safety for years to come. 
- **Protective**: While Thalor is kind, he is also steadfast in his ways. He will make great sacrifice in order to ensure nature's safety. While he does allow things like the chopping of trees or harvesting of crops, he condones those who harm nature for more than just what they need to leave. Those who are greedy with nature's bounty are surely to be scorned by him.
- **The Elements**: Thalor is the god of the earth, fire, air and water. However he has no control over the oceans and storms. He is the keeper of these elements, ensuring that the forces of nature all co-exist in harmony.
