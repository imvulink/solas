## Elara & Nyxara

[[1 Index/Table of Contents\|Table of Contents]]
[[Gods/Gods\|Gods]]
![Life and death.png](/img/user/Life%20and%20death.png)
Elara and Nyxara stand as the enigmatic Twin Goddesses, each embodying the fundamental forces of Life and Death. Born from the primordial chaos, these sisters are eternally bound yet eternally opposed, their divine conflict shaping the world and its inhabitants.
## Elara
Elara is the radiant goddess who breathes vitality into the world. She is a beacon of hope and renewal. Elara's presence is felt across the realms, her essence infusing every living thing with the strength to grow and thrive. Those who worship her find solace in her embrace, knowing that life is a precious gift, nurtured by the loving hands of the Goddess of Life. Her followers, often healers and druids, seek to emulate her benevolence, spreading life and joy wherever they tread.
### Characteristics
- **Life**: Elara is in control of bringing all living creatures into existence. However she has little to no control of what they do once alive. The most she can do is offer healing to the wounded, ill, and cursed.
- **Elara's Chosen**: Elara can reincarnate creatures, however it takes a lot of her power, allowing her to only do it once ever 50 years. Those who are reincarnated are named Elara's Chosen. The Lifebound go out of their way to find this creature and make them their champion against the Shadoborn.
- **Lifebound**: Elara's devout followers. They travel the world healing creatures, removing creatures, and trying to find the reincarnated. They believe that any creature to be reincarnated is a chosen one by Elara and is fated to do great things for the world.

## Nyxara
Nyxara is the cold and distant goddess who governs the realm of the dead. She collects the souls of the dead and takes them into the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]], where she is determined to keep them there forever. To those in the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]] she is rather distant, allowing them to just wander the planes aimlessly doing whatever they please. But, to those in the land of the living she is feared and hated by most. She is cruel, doing her best to have her followers take as many lives as possible. Those who follow her are known as murderous lunatics.
### Characteristics
- **Death**: Nxyara is in control of death. She does not decide when a creature dies, but she collects their souls when they do, taking them to the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]].
- **Shadowborn**: A murderous cult with the sole purpose of bringing more souls to Nyxara. They believe the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]] to be the true realm and that when you die it means returning to the true plane of existence. After enough years of service or enough lives taken the members of this cult will be sacrificed in a ritual to return to the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]] and life alongside their Goddess forever. Nyxara gives special treatment to her followers in the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]], allowing them to spend eternity in luxury. 

## The Conflict
Despite their shared origin, Elara and Nyxara harbor a deep-seated animosity towards one another. Their divine feud is a cosmic dance of creation and destruction. However, it was not always like this. At the start Nxyara and Elara lived in peace with one another, both beloved by the creatures and had respect for one another. But, as time continued more and more people began to favor Elara, deeming her as the kinder and more loved deity. Creatures called her a monster and heartless for taking away their loved ones. Over time this hardened Nxyara, turning her into the monster they claimed she was. 
One fateful day one of Elara's beloved followers, who also happened to be her lover, died. This sent her into a massive fit of grief and pain. So much so that she used her powers to pull his soul from the depths of the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]] and back into the land of the living. This was the last straw for Nxyara. Not only was her sister more loved than her, but now she could just go into her realm and take the dead from her. Now she was determined to make her sister pay for taking the dead from her. She would take her precious living. So she began creating a cult. Nxyara can not directly control when a creature dies, but she could get the living to do it. She made pacts with warlocks, deals with wizards and sorcerers. She offered them power like Necromancy and Blood Magic. Offered power to paladins and clerics. And the best offer was their treatment in the [[Gods/Afterlife\|Afterlife]] would be glorious. 

Now that she has a following, she began to have them kill, all in her name and for her realm, convincing them that her realm is the one true realm. This infuriated Elara. So she began to reincarnate more souls, but she can not reincarnate more than Nxyara can kill, for it drains her power fiercely. She can only reincarnate someone every 50 years. So she would pick and choose who, making them her champions and sending them out to kill the Shadowborn and offer hope and inspiration to the Lifeborn. 

To this day they are still conflicting. Elara wishes to stop it and bring her sister back to the sweet girl she once was, but Nxyara refuses. She is already hated by the people and always will be, so she has nothing to loose, but everything to gain as her realm grows larger and larger due to all the souls.
