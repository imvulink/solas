# 3-22-25
[[1 Index/Table of Contents\|Table of Contents]]
Brought the villagers back to Salmyre

Set everyone up at the Inn

Met Roland and talked about going to Calbridge

Circe flirted with Roland

Had a moment with Xena and Evar about the Isle and the princess possibly being there. They both said no. 

Met with Mystra who did a tarot reading.

Traveled to Calbridge

Fought some ice creatures

Found a strange bag of holding that seems to react to blood. 

Made it into town. Had an interaction with Mariya and said they were merchants. 

Ran into Roxyana. Her and Varin fought. Roxyana told him where to find one of the mercenaries that attacked his village.

Confronted mercenary and got some more info.

Roxyana revealed she has the contract from the Prince of Varin's Village about him planning the attack.

Gives it to Varin under the terms of him freeing the people who are captured by the guards. 

The party goes to find the guards barracks. Belic is arrested. 

Party finds the training grounds and sees people being beaten and threatened into listening and obeying orders. 

Break into the Barracks. Varin and Xena team up. Keep failing rolls on the back staircase. They get caught by Mariya and get kicked out.

Blaze and Circe team up and manage to find the prisoners. They convince the guard on duty to leave to get wood so Blaze can fix the fire place. They break out all of the prisoners. They send everyone who wasn't from Hedron on their way and keep the other's in Blaze's bracelet.

They journey to Varin's village. Varin is shunned by them. His mother meets with him and they talk. Varin shows her the contract and she gives him a memory potion.

Varin decides to use the potion on the prince.

They confront the prince at the temple. Varin and him duel. The prince is getting his ass kicked by Varin and pulls out black magic. The black magic causes his axe to awaken. 

They bring the prince back to the main hall. Varin's mother uses the potion on the prince and shows everyone the true story. The prince set up the attack on the village to frame Varin. Varin is finally unshamed and welcomed back home.
