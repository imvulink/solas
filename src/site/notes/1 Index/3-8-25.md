## Session 3-8-25
[[1 Index/Table of Contents\|Table of Contents]]

- Went into the Adventurers Guild to get a quest.
- Picked up a quest to go kill some rats in Hedron that were killing people and stealing food. 
- Had Ota (Merchant from last session) Bring them to Hedron.
- On the way they were jumped by bushes and vines made from belladonna. 
- Ota was attacked. they saved him but failed to realize he was poisoned. He died in the morning. 
- That night at camp everyone hung out. Xena and Varin shared a tent and got a bit closer uwu.
- The next morning Ota was found dead. Circe realized Varin was poisoned.
- Took Ota's cart as their own and hurried to Hedron. 
- They were met with guards arguing with the leader of Hedron, Darnouk, over how few people he was letting them take. 
- A kid came out crying for his mommy and was kicked out the way, getting knocked out as he hit a cart. 
- The guards left with the people they wanted after Darnouk convinced the party not to fight. 
- Went inside and were told about the giant rats that were eating the food and killing people. 
- Ernest guided them to the windmill and through the first parts of it. 
- Fought the giant rats and made it through the caverns. 
- Came across a toxic pond. Fought the slime monster that was corrupting it.
- Toxic monster created a cave in that killed Ernest. 
- Party left the cavern and went back to the town hall. Were telling Darnouk about the adventure.
- Same guards from before showed up and the party started a fight at Darnouks request. The kids started throwing rocks at the guards. 
- During the fighting 2 of the guards died. The captain left pissed, threatening to come back.
- Party took the rest of the town folk with them in Blaze's bracelet. 
- Enjoyed a night at camp.
