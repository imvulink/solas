### Backstory
In her early life Circe was raised by Shadowborn, a cult built by and dedicated to Nyxara, Goddess of Death. She spent her days learning their creed and magic, following their rules and behaving as told - making sure to excel where she could so that her believed mother would look good to the other cultists. While still young, her curiosity of the world grew to become almost insatiable, a trait her believed mother tried to squash the minute it showed in fear it would lead her to leave. Learning to search for information when no one was awake, Circe discovered documents detailing a ritual where her name was mentioned several times alongside an undisclosed death. Feeling unsafe and fearful for the first time in her life, she searched for more information where she discovered her true parentage, their location and status both unknown. 

Not wanting to become a sacrifice, no matter how loyal to them she appeared, the seed of doubt lead Circe to gather what little she owned and slip out into the night. Within a week of her being on her own, she was discovered by a horned man who claimed to be a runaway Shadowborn as well. His eyes and tone were kind as he helped her find shelter and food, and as the years passed her distrust of him grew to familial love. Sindar, the man she now called father, taught her survival and control over her magic - as well as control over some less appealing personality traits of hers that became apparent as she grew. Circe did what she could to aid him in their safety, but she soon discovered they would never be truly safe. She would never truly be safe. 

A single night was all it took, the night of her true birthday, the Shadowborn found them and laid waste to their camp. In their rush to escape the cultists, Sindar was caught and killed before her eyes, causing something in her to snap. As she screamed for her adoptive father, the cultists who ventured too close burst into a bloody mist as her magic ran rampant. Feral and terrified, she ran for her safety, only stopping when she reached a seemingly safe town she learned was called Carlens. Rubbing mud into her hair to disguise the color, she kept to alleyways and shadows, doing her best to stay out of sight in fear of the cultists finding her again. Odd and dirty jobs became her livelihood, willing to do anything for the sake of coin until she had enough for a comfortable lifestyle
